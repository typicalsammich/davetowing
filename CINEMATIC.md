# Back in Motion — kinetic brand opening

The current introduction is a 6.3-second CSS motion-design sequence. It replaces the parked-truck WebGL scene entirely at runtime.

1. Perspective road markings move toward the viewer while ROAD / STOPS. enters the frame.
2. A full-width photographic shutter sweeps in with ONE / CALL. typography and a moving gold scan line.
3. A gold screen rises and the DAVE. wordmark moves across it.
4. The selected company logo locks into place, BACK IN / MOTION. reveals, and the full composition lifts away into the homepage.

No WebGL canvas, 3D model, video service or new image asset is loaded. The road is a graphic treatment, and the truck photograph appears as part of the motion-design sequence. This is a stylized brand opening.

Immediate Skip Intro, Replay Intro, Escape, session playback control, reduced-motion support, save-data bypass and a completion timeout remain enabled. The header phone CTA remains available throughout. A new session key lets returning visitors see the redesigned opening once.

Desktop/mobile playback, text fit, skip and replay were checked in the browser with no console errors. Controller checks cover autoplay, animation completion, child-animation event isolation, skip, replay, Escape, session suppression, reduced motion, save-data and timeout behavior.
